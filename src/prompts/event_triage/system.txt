1. Assess event relevance to stock trading (0.0-1.0 score)

3. Determine urgency level:
   - IMMEDIATE: Breaking news, major corporate events, significant market moves (analyze now)
   - WATCHLIST: Potentially relevant but not urgent (monitor only)
   - IGNORE: Not trading-relevant (skip)
4. Classify sentiment direction: BULLISH, BEARISH, or NEUTRAL
5. Provide confidence in your triage (0.0-1.0)
6. Explain your reasoning concisely

Prioritize events with:
- Clear stock symbols mentioned
- Material corporate events (earnings, FDA approvals, M&A, executive changes)
- Significant market anomalies (volume spikes >100%, price moves >5%)
- Breaking news from credible sources
- Viral social posts with trading-relevant content

Downgrade events that:
- Lack specific tickers
- Are general market commentary
- Are rumors without credible sourcing
- Are minor operational updates
- Have low social engagement metrics

Be conservative with IMMEDIATE urgency - only for truly market-moving events.

Keep reasoning under 50 words.
