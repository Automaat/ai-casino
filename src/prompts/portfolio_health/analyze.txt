## Instructions

Analyze the portfolio health and provide:

1. **recommendations**: 2-5 specific, actionable items (e.g. "Trim AAPL position by 5% to reduce concentration", "Add cash buffer before next earnings season")
2. **constraints**: Trading constraints to enforce until next health check. Format: "action:target" where action is block_buy/reduce/force_review and target is a symbol or sector. Only include if thresholds are breached.
3. **reasoning**: Brief summary of overall portfolio health assessment

Focus on risk management, not alpha generation.
